# SunnyVerse Intelligent Applications Master Series | AB-410

**17 modules · 4 learning paths · 2 complementary podcast shows · timestamped learning companions**

Welcome to **SunnyVerse AI Labs** — an independent educational YouTube and podcast brand. This public repository helps you learn alongside the **Intelligent Applications Master Series** through original examples, timestamped podcast references, and follow-along Power Platform instructions when a lesson genuinely includes hands-on activities.

> **Independent educational companion.** This is not an official Microsoft training repository or Microsoft-endorsed certification material. The fictional learning organization is **SunnyVerse Renewable Group**. Products, interfaces, feature availability, and licensing may change.

## Start here

Read the [learner quick-start](docs/START-HERE.md), choose a module below, play the matching podcast and use its timestamp links to follow along. A **listening companion** explains concepts without inventing exercises; a **build-along guide** provides steps, values, verification, and troubleshooting when the taught lesson actually includes practical operations.

**Two shows:**
- **Twinkle & Celestyn — Design & Troubleshooting Lab:** concepts, configuration, verification, and troubleshooting.
- **Arka & Veda — Enterprise Architecture & Innovation Lab:** architecture, alternatives, governance, economics, and scale.

## The SunnyVerse learning world

Examples use the fictional **SunnyVerse Renewable Group** across **Wind Operations**, **Solar Operations**, **Hydropower Operations**, **Battery Storage Operations**, and **Data Center Energy & Sustainability**. All records and activities are learning scenarios, not instructions for live infrastructure operations.

## Use a companion

1. Open the module page.
2. Start the Twinkle & Celestyn episode and use the clickable YouTube timestamps to jump to relevant sections.
3. For hands-on topics, follow the guide's actual build actions and check expected results.
4. Refer to Arka & Veda for the enterprise perspective.
5. Use a learning environment and fictional data. Never upload customer secrets or real operational records.
