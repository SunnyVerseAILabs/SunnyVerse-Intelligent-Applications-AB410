Open a module page beside the podcast. Click a blue timestamp to jump to the corresponding passage. Pause when an activity requires attention, then return to the audio.

**M01 and M02 contain no formal hands-on lab.** They provide conceptual explanations, illustrated renewable-energy scenarios, and practical decision references. Later module guides will include precise configuration steps, test evidence, and troubleshooting only when the recorded and official lessons teach those actions.

## Practice safely

Use a non-production learning environment when future modules require Power Apps or Power Automate. Each guide will identify any environment, permission, licensing, data, and verification requirements. Never use real industrial control data, customer information, secrets or live operational systems in the fictional exercises.

## Two complementary shows

**Twinkle & Celestyn:** Understand it, design it, validate it, troubleshoot it.  
**Arka & Veda:** Design it, justify it, compare it, govern it, scale it.

The teaching organization, SunnyVerse Renewable Group, is fictional. This course is independently authored and not affiliated with Microsoft.
